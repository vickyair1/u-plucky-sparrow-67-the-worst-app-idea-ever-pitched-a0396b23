# The Worst App Idea Ever Pitched

One sentence pitching the worst, most useless app imaginable — a product that sounds real, a founder who sounds certain, and a function nobody on earth needs.

> Pebble is the world's first app that lets you adopt, name, and emotionally support a single rock in your garden, with daily check-ins and a grief counselor for when it erodes.

The pitch is deadpan founder voice all the way down: a named product, a specific useless function, and a problem nobody has — a rock that needs emotional support — solved with total confidence, daily check-ins and a grief counselor included. Nothing in it winks at the reader; the founder means every word, and that is exactly what makes it perfectly pointless.
